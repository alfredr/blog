Parajudica is a reasoning system for assessing how data is classified under different legal and organizational frameworks. This is joint work with Luc Moreau and Sophie Stalla-Bourdillon.

The system represents data, context, and rules in RDF and uses SPARQL to derive classifications. It accounts for relationships between datasets and preserves the results of different frameworks, allowing their interpretations to be compared. The paper illustrates this through healthcare data and de-identification scenarios.
